| 2026-09-16 | **Call-only retry gating as final protocol** | (a) Unconstrained retry (`--retry-mode any`): inject error on any step-1 response;<br>(b) Call-only retry (`--retry-mode call-only`): retry step 1 only if at least one tool call was parsed and denied by contract. | On live R4 evaluations, 96 of 300 samples produced step-1 prose responses (refusals/explanations).
