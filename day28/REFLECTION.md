# REFLECTION — five-minute mission reflection (Day 28)

**Mission.** Convert Q1–Q5 into one rigorous, findings-first technical argument.

**Fail condition.** A published claim has no committed result or honest
limitation. — *Not triggered:* all 29 substantive paragraphs are registered,
every source resolves to a Git blob at the current revision, numeric claims bind
to exact JSON pointers, and each non-method claim names at least one of eight
published limitations.

**The answer.** Recovery is not a binary feature. The defensible outcome is
correct user-visible success inside cost and latency limits. Depth, detection,
retries, fallback, and policy composition each show a different way that a
single headline can hide failure.

**The sharpest editorial decision.** Audit interpretations, not only numbers.
The article fails if a substantive paragraph appears without a claim marker.
This closes the space where an accurate chart can acquire an unsupported causal
story in surrounding prose.

**The hardest defense.** P4 must remain a reference upper bound. It uses perfect
retryability truth and a strict simulator oracle, while the measured semantic
judge is explicitly not standalone validated. The article carries that caveat in
the finding, figure caption, limitation, market checklist, and checkpoint.

**The visual lesson.** One figure per research question is enough. Each one shows
the decision-changing relationship, while its caption names the population,
comparison, result, and boundary needed to read it alone.

**Mastery gate.**

- **Can explain** — the article turns five results into one conditional product
  claim.
- **Can build** — deterministic article evidence, five SVGs, manifests, and a
  one-command generator.
- **Can debug** — the audit identifies the exact paragraph, token, pointer,
  caption, figure, source, or limitation that drifted.
- **Can measure** — Wilson, bootstrap, McNemar, paired seeds, cost, latency, and
  attacks retain their original units.
- **Can defend** — 29 claims, eight limitations, five source-linked figures, and
  Checkpoint 28 are machine checked.

**What I would study next.** Replace the P4 simulator-truth routing and oracle
guard with measured classifiers, then re-run the same constraint-first selection
with their errors, cost, latency, and subgroup failure slices included.
