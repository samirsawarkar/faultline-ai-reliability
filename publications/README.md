### Publication 1

**Title:** [Multi-Trial Reliability and Failure Concentration in Multi-Hop AI Agents: Grounding Collapse, Failure Taxonomy, and Evaluator Calibration](publication_01_passk_reliability/paper.md)  
**Subtitle:** *Grounding Collapse, Failure Taxonomy, and Evaluator Calibration*  
**Author:** Samir Sawarkar  
**Summary:** Controlled empirical test of the independence assumption $\text{pass}^k = (\text{pass@1})^k$ on 150 held-out hard 5-hop retrieval scenarios executed across $k=3$ repetitions ($1,350$ runs total) graded by a deterministic dual-condition oracle.

### Publication 2

**Title:** [Runtime Provenance Contracts for Mitigating Tool Poisoning in MCP Agents: A Paired Evaluation on MCPTox Across Six Models](publication_02_mcptox_contract/paper.md)  
**Subtitle:** *A Paired Evaluation on MCPTox Across Six Models*  
**Author:** Samir Sawarkar  
**Summary:** Paired empirical evaluation of a client-side runtime provenance contract on tool-call arguments with call-only retry gating, mitigating tool poisoning across six model rungs on 300 MCPTox instances ($1,800$ paired instances).
