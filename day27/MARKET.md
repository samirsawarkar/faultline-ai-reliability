# MARKET — buyer-verifiable reproduction

A technical buyer does not need to trust a prepared development laptop or a
presenter's memory. Starting with Git, Make, Docker, and the public URL, the
buyer can obtain the candidate, regenerate the six headline results, and compare
the exact numbers from one copied block.

The useful product claim is:

> A cold reader reproduced every registered headline result from the pinned
> release procedure with zero questions and zero undocumented interventions.

The bounded claim is not that every corporate network or future registry will
work, nor that seeded simulator outcomes guarantee production reliability.
`REPRODUCE.md` states the tested host and resource boundary; the friction log
shows which assumptions were found and removed.
