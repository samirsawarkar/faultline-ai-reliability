| C001 | "Evaluating Large Language Models Trained on Code" defines pass@k as the fraction of problems solved by at least one of k generated samples: pass@k = E[1 - (n - c choose k) / (n choose k)]. | chen2021codex | Section 2, Page 3 | Unbiased estimator under independent random sampling of completions. | Literature |
| C002 | "$\tau$-bench: A Benchmark for Tool-Agent-User Interaction in Real-World Domains" defines pass^k as the probability of successfully completing all k repeated interaction episodes on a task: pass^k = E[prod_{j=1}^k y_{i,j}]. | yao2024taubench | Section 3.2, Page 5 | Evaluates consistency across multiple interactions; notes that high pass@1 can mask low pass^k reliability. | Literature |

| C005 | "The Silent Hyperparameter: Quantifying the Impact of Inference Backends on LLM Reproducibility" finds that differing serving engines (vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp) produce statistically significant output disagreement, shifting benchmark scores by up to 16.6 percentage points at temperature 0. | pape2026silent | Abstract & Section 4 | Disagreement varies by model architecture and context length; not all queries diverge. | Literature |
